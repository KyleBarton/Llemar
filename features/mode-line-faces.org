separators, =success= for User, =font-lock-keyword-face= for Assistant/tools,

(=erc-nick-default-face= versus =erc-my-nick-face=/=erc-prompt-face=). Revisit
participant identity versus operational state when choosing inheritance, but
avoid an ERC dependency. Keep the approved styling until real use informs this.
